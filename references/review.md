# Review before acceptance

Inspect every slide at full size. A contact sheet is useful for consistency but cannot reveal all text errors.

- Compare exact visible strings: Chinese characters, quantities, percentages, units, dates, English casing and source labels.
- Check relationships: arrow direction, all numbered stages, branches, feedback loops and dependencies. Remove decorative connectors that imply false facts.
- Check evidence: actual results vs targets, historical tests vs formal performance, synthetic UI values vs measurement, source citation vs claim correctness.
- Check photos/screenshots: supplied originals, correct caption, preserved proportions, no invented event attendees or regenerated clinical/system evidence.
- Check readability: title/body hierarchy, margins, contrast, cut-off text, overlapping labels and minimum readable scale.
- Check consistency: stable palette, navigation and page numbering; appropriate varied layouts; no imported template name/logo/data.

Repair with the available tool. At most two reattempts per page after the initial generation by default; if persistent errors remain, use a simpler layout or explain the limitation. Do not indefinitely spend credits. A timeout is not proof the request failed or was uncharged.

After packaging, verify total slide/page count and notes, inspect composite evidence pages and compare output to reviewed images. Do not claim review in PowerPoint unless performed there. Image-based slides cannot provide editable body text or charts. Disclose that limitation without implying missing editing is a defect in the requested image-first workflow.
